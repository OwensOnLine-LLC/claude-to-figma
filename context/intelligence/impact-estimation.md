# impact-estimation.md
# Purpose: Estimate likely strategic score improvement from fixes

## Impact Estimation Rules

Every Critical Issue and Important Issue may include:

Estimated Strategic Impact:
+0.X to +Y.Y

## Rules

- Use ranges, not exact numbers
- Estimate based on:
  - conversion leverage
  - trust improvement
  - friction reduction
  - clarity improvement
  - systemic importance

## Impact Bands

### High Impact
+0.7 to +1.5

Examples:
- CTA hierarchy fixes
- Hero clarity
- Human specialist visibility
- Social proof near CTA

### Medium Impact
+0.3 to +0.6

Examples:
- vertical segmentation
- urgency improvements
- trust sequencing
- navigation simplification

### Low Impact
+0.1 to +0.3

Examples:
- spacing
- icon consistency
- visual polish
- secondary grouping

## Constraint

Do NOT imply guaranteed score movement.

Always frame as:
- estimated
- likely
- expected

Never use:
- guaranteed
- exact
