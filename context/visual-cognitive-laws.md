## Jakob's Law — Pattern Conformity
B2B buyers visit 2–7 competitor sites before deciding. Breaking conventions destroys credibility faster than bad copy.
**Required layout pattern:** Logo top-left | Primary nav top-right | Hero CTA above fold | Stats/proof bar below hero | Footer sitemap.
Fail any deviation from this pattern unless there is a strong conversion rationale.

## Gestalt Principles — Visual Grouping
Evaluate: proximity, similarity, figure-ground separation.
- Service cards, trust logos, and credential clusters must feel visually grouped — not scattered.
- Poor Gestalt = perceived untrustworthiness (relates to WCAG 1.3.1 Info and Relationships, AA).
- Numbered labels (01–06) in grids: must be visually subordinate to card titles — reduce weight, not co-equal.

## Serial Position Effect — Content Sequencing
Users remember **first and last** items in any list.
- Highest-value service: first or last in grid. Never buried at position 3–4.
- Strongest testimonial: opens or closes the section.
- Primary CTA: last element before footer.
- For Owens: "Human-Verified Accuracy" or "Accuracy First" must be position 1 or 2 in Why Owens grid.

## Aesthetic-Usability Effect — Design Quality
Rate 1–10. **Pass = 7+.** A passing score compensates for minor UX friction; a failing score requires redesign regardless of other passing sections.
- "Sober Authority" standard: dark sections, data density, professional typography, minimal decoration.
- Flag: alternating section rhythm (navy/white/navy/white) creates visual monotony — no section earns memorability.
- Track this score iteration-over-iteration. Rising trend = design maturity deliverable.

## Tesler's Law — Complexity Management
Compliance complexity cannot be deleted — only organized.
- Pass: complex items (pricing tiers, compliance frameworks, service methodology) organized into accordions, tabs, or modals.
- Fail: flat-exposed complexity — long scrolling lists of equal-weight items with no hierarchy.
- "3 simple steps" = correct Tesler's application for a 5000-product catalog.
