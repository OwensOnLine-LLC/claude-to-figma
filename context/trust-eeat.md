## ⚑ Master Criterion — Human Specialist Network
**The logo-swap test:** Could a buyer replace the Owens logo with a competitor's and notice no meaningful difference in trust communication in the first viewport? If YES → Master Criterion Advisory Flag (does not hard-FAIL if claim exists anywhere on page; hard FAIL if claim is entirely absent from all visible content).

**Pass:** Specialist count, investigator imagery, methodology statement, or in-country expertise visible in hero, stats bar, or services section.
**Advisory:** Claim exists only in body copy — not section-level visible.
**Hard FAIL:** No human expert signal anywhere on the page. Triggers automatic GEO/Trust FAIL on Trust Architecture section. Must be remediated before any other recommendation is prioritized.

## E-E-A-T Framework (Trustworthiness = Most Weighted)

**Experience (E — Owens' strongest signal):**
First-hand, lived proof. Case studies, real client outcomes, 34-year operational history (Founded 1992), authentic team/office imagery — not stock photos. Milestone timeline counts.

**Expertise (E):**
Credentials, certifications, methodology visible: ISO 27001, IAPP, ASIS, SHRM, FCRA, KYC/AML methodology.

**Authoritativeness (A):**
Industry logos, analyst ratings (Gartner/Forrester), media mentions, "Founded 1992" permanence signal. Absence of analyst rating = highest-authority gap.

**Trustworthiness (T — Most Weighted):**
- 3-Second Check: "30+ Years" + industry logos above fold.
- Zero named testimonials = Tier T critical gap.
- Dashboard/SaaS screenshots weaken T — skew impression toward "automated."
- Banned words scan (see language-rules.md).
- External validation: G2/Capterra/BBB badge. Trade association logos alone insufficient.
