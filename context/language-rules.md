## Banned Words (Binary FAIL — any instance on any page)
- innovative
- world-class
- cutting-edge
- leverage (as business verb)
- synergies

**Audit action:** Scan all visible copy. One instance = FAIL. Provide exact location and replacement copy.

**Common replacement patterns:**
- "world-class verification" → "in-country verified screening, personally reviewed by our specialist network"
- "innovative platform" → "background screening platform, built on 34 years of operational expertise"
- "cutting-edge technology" → "proprietary screening infrastructure across 250+ jurisdictions"

## Required Language (Homepage / Hero — Binary FAIL if absent)
| Term | Where Required | Status Check |
|---|---|---|
| ISO 27001 Certified | Hero or certifications section | Badge = pass. Logo only = partial. |
| 250+ Countries Coverage | H1 or hero stat bar | Must be explicit number, not "global" |
| IAPP Accredited | Hero, partner bar, or certifications | Logo = pass |
| Founded 1992 | Header, hero, or "Our Story" | Must appear above fold or in hero area |
| Human Expert Verification | Hero, stats bar, or services section | Body-text-only = advisory flag |

## Interior Page Override
On interior/content pages, required hero language is NOT required. Replace with:
- **Page headline clarity:** H1 describes exactly what the page delivers.
- **Banned words scan:** Still applies on all pages.

## Nielsen Real-World Language Standard
Copy must match B2B buyer vocabulary — not internal jargon or marketing fluff. Test: would a Chief Compliance Officer at a global bank say this phrase in a meeting? If not, flag it.

Owens-specific buyer vocabulary:
✓ Due diligence | Background screening | In-country verification | Compliance-ready | Regulatory risk | KYC/AML | GDPR/FCRA/LGPD
✗ "Hire with confidence" | "Got Timeline? We got you." | "Simply better"
