## Gartner 2025 — Loose Influencer Network Model
B2B purchases involve ~10 stakeholders operating as a **loose influencer network**, not a formal sequential committee. Informal champions and hidden blockers carry as much weight as named decision-makers. Map each page section to the stakeholder it serves.

## Six Personas — Owens OnLine Homepage

| Persona | Served By | Pass Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Executive / C-Level** | Hero: ROI, global authority, risk reduction | Above fold — must be first |
| **Finance / Procurement** | Pricing clarity, "No minimums, no contract," "Founded 1992" | Hero or immediately below |
| **Compliance / Legal** | GDPR/FCRA/LGPD, certifications, audit trails | Dedicated section or stats bar |
| **IT / Security** | ISO 27001, data handling, infrastructure, encryption | Dedicated section |
| **End User / Operations** | Turnaround speed, ease of use, 3-step process | Workflow/process section |
| **Informal Influencer / Research Lead** | Comparison table, methodology brief, differentiation proof | Case studies / downloadable assets |

Flag any persona with **zero homepage coverage**. The Informal Influencer is most commonly absent — they shortlist vendors before the committee meets and need shareable evidence to champion Owens internally.

## Two-Gate Clarity Test (Sequential — Trust First)

**Gate 1 — 1.7-Second Scan (Brand Sentiment & Rational Trust):**
Research: users form subconscious judgment in 1.7 seconds — not "What do they do?" but "Do I trust this?" (NN/g 2025; 38% abandon after 50ms poor impression).
- Pass: visual authority, professional density, recognizable trust markers visible in first viewport without reading.
- Fail: generic, interchangeable with automated competitor, stock-photo-heavy.
- **A design that fails Gate 1 cannot pass Gate 2.**

**Gate 2 — 5-Second Clarity Test (Functional Clarity):**
After Gate 1 passes, evaluate function. Three questions answered in 5 seconds:
1. What does this company do?
2. Who do they serve?
3. Why choose Owens over a competitor?

Both gates must pass. Report as: "Gate 1: [score] → Gate 2: [score]."
