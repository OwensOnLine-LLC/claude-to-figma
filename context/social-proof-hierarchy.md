## Effectiveness Ranking (Highest → Lowest Conversion Impact)
1. **Analyst Ratings** — Gartner Magic Quadrant, Forrester Wave. Highest authority for B2B.
2. **Quantified Case Studies** — "Reduced screening time by 40%." Must include metric.
3. **Named Testimonials** — Name + Title + Company + outcome. Anonymous = Tier 6, not Tier 3.
4. **Certifications** — SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, IAPP. Operational credibility, not outcome proof.
5. **Awards / Press Mentions** — "As seen in SHRM, HR Executive." Third-party recognition.
6. **Client / Partner Logo Bars** — Association logos. Trust signal, not proof of results.

## Placement Standards (Homepage / Type 1)
- **Logo bar above fold** — Required. (+111% conversion per Basecamp research)
- **Testimonials adjacent to primary CTA** — Not buried in a separate section.
- **Certifications grouped and prominent** — Not scattered in footer.
- **Minimum 3 trust signals visible without scrolling.**
- **External review badge** (G2/Capterra/BBB) above fold — one badge provides third-party validation that certifications alone cannot.

## Interior Page Override (Type 1B — Contextual Proof Only)
Logo bar above fold is **not required** on interior pages. Replace with:
- 1 contextually relevant trust signal **near the primary CTA** on this specific page.
- Examples: certification badge near a compliance claim, a stat near a product feature, a short testimonial adjacent to a specific service.
- Minimum: 1 contextual proof signal. Absence = flag, not fail.

## Common Audit Gaps (Owens Specific)
- **Named testimonials:** Most consistently absent across all page types. Single highest-impact addition.
- **Analyst rating:** Absent entirely. Even a G2 badge moves the social proof stack significantly.
- **Case study entry points:** No quantified outcomes anywhere — "4000+ Global Clients" is anonymous.
- **Press logos:** "As seen in" bar requires zero editorial process — collect existing mentions.

## Scoring Note
Social proof section scored 1–10. Missing Tier 1–3 entirely = Flag (5–6) or Fail (4 or below) regardless of Tier 4–6 presence.
